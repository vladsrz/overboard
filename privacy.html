Privacy

Play activity, device type and country are collected. No names.

What is sent

  • That someone opened the game, and when a match finishes (rounds, falls, pickups, who won — seats are “person” or a bot, never a typed name).
  • A random id made in this browser, so repeat visits can be counted without knowing who you are.
  • Rough device type (touch or not) and country, from the network path to the game server.

What is not

  • Names, room codes you type, or anything else you enter.
  • IP addresses in the play record.

Why

To see how the game is played and tune it. These counts are not for prizes or rankings.

On this device

A longer record of matches stays in this browser’s local storage so you can review it at dev/stats.html on the same address. Clearing site data clears it.
